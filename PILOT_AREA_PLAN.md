## 1. Pilot Area

* **Locality / Neighborhood:** Koramangala (Specifically Blocks 4, 5, and 6), Bengaluru, Karnataka, India
* **Geographic Center:** $12.9340^\circ\text{ N}, 77.6220^\circ\text{ E}$ (Around 80 Feet Road / 5th Block Junction)
* **Terrain & Mobility Context:** Dense commercial dining corridor flanked by residential pockets and welfare homes. Paved municipal roads, high two-wheeler navigability, predictable traffic patterns post-21:30.
* **Why this area is ideal for Pilot Phase 0:**
  1. High concentration of mid-sized sit-down restaurants within walking and short scooter distance (5–10 minutes transit).
  2. Established community shelters and orphanages located within a 1.5 to 2.5 km perimeter.
  3. Compact enough that if the mobile application, network connection, or server crashes entirely, the coordinator can call the donor, volunteer, and shelter directly and walk/ride between points within 15 minutes.

---

## 2. Pilot Donor Type

* **Selected Primary Category:** **Sit-Down & Casual Dining Restaurants** (Specializing in South Indian / North Indian Thalis, Curries, Rice Dishes, and Bread)
* **Excluded Categories for Pilot:**
  * *Wedding / Banquet Halls:* Excluded due to erratic scheduling, unpredictable dates, and massive sudden surplus (150–500 meals) requiring four-wheeler mini-trucks.
  * *College Canteens:* Excluded due to vacation calendars, student exam periods, and institutional tender bureaucracy.
  * *Luxury 5-Star Hotels:* Excluded due to lengthy corporate legal clearance and strict brand risk management.
  * *Street Food Carts:* Excluded due to variable hygiene consistency and lack of commercial hot-holding or cold-storage facilities.
* **Why Casual Dining Restaurants work best:**
  * Consistent daily dinner service ending between 22:00 and 22:45.
  * Manageable batch surplus sizes (15 to 35 meals per evening) easily transported in standard thermal insulated delivery backpacks.
  * Kitchens possess clean food-grade stainless steel vessels or takeout containers and understand temperature preservation (refrigeration/hot holding).

## 9. Communication Method

To guarantee zero-failure operations where technical glitches never strand edible food:

1. **Primary Digital Channel:**
   * Mobile Application (Donor logs donation $\to$ NGO/Volunteer receives real-time notification $\to$ Donor displays 6-digit OTP $\to$ Volunteer enters OTP on touch keypad $\to$ Status transitions to `collected` $\to$ Delivered).
2. **Synchronized Coordination Layer:**
   * A dedicated **"Koramangala Food Rescue Pilot — Ops Room"** WhatsApp group consisting of:
     * The Lead Pilot Coordinator
     * The 2 Restaurant Kitchen Managers
     * The 2 NGO Resident Caretakers
     * The 3 Volunteer Riders
3. **Manual Fallback Protocol (If App/Internet Fails):**
   * If the app fails to dispatch or crashes:
     1. Donor calls Pilot Coordinator at 22:15.
     2. Coordinator dials Primary Volunteer (Rahul) directly.
     3. Volunteer arrives at restaurant, calls Coordinator to confirm verbal handoff code.
     4. Food is delivered to Green Hope Shelter; Coordinator manually logs the rescue event in the backend web console.

## 10. Pilot Risks & Concrete Mitigations

| # | Identified Real-World Risk | Probability | Severity | Practical Field Mitigation |
|---|---|:---:|:---:|---|
| 1 | **Food Temperature / Microbiological Decay**<br/>Food held at room temperature spoils before consumption. | Low | Critical | Only cooked food prepared after 19:30 is accepted. Donor must affirm 5-point safety declaration. Transport occurs in insulated thermal bags within a 45-minute window. |
| 2 | **Dietary Mismatch**<br/>Shelter accepts vegetarian food only, but donor has surplus chicken gravy. | Medium | High | Dietary tags (`VEG` / `NON_VEG`) are strictly confirmed in the donation payload. If non-veg, task automatically routes to Karunai Night Shelter which accepts all categories. |
| 3 | **Late Restaurant Closure**<br/>Restaurant kitchen stays open past 23:00 due to late customer surge; no surplus available. | Medium | Medium | Restaurant manager sends a "No Surplus Tonight" confirmation ping on WhatsApp by 22:00. Volunteers are stood down with no wasted trips. |
| 4 | **Volunteer No-Show / Vehicle Breakdown**<br/>Primary courier gets flat tire or is unreachable at 22:15. | Low | High | Proactive dispatch auto-escalates to Volunteer 2 (Priya) after 8 minutes. If unavailable, Green Hope Shelter executes a direct 850m walk/scooter self-pickup. |
| 5 | **Packaging Inadequacy / Spillage in Transit**<br/>Gravy or sambar leaks during two-wheeler transport. | Medium | Medium | Donor is briefed during onboarding to pack food in tightly sealed food-grade containers or double-bagged poly-pouches provided by the pilot program. Couriers carry vertical divider bags. |
| 6 | **OTP Forgotten or Phone Dead at Handover**<br/>Donor floor manager's smartphone runs out of battery before revealing OTP. | Low | Medium | Coordinator can verify the handover via secondary phone call and execute an administrative state override in the Web Operations Console with mandatory audit logs. |
